# SpineVision User Walkthrough

Welcome to SpineVision, the ultimate ecosystem for book resellers. This guide will walk you through the core workflows based on your membership tier.

## 1. Getting Started
- **Onboarding**: All users start with a quick scan of their first book using OmniVision.
- **Profile Setup**: Connect your marketplace accounts (Amazon, eBay) in NexusVision.

## 2. Membership Tiers

### A. Free Tier (Hobbyist)
- **OmniVision**: Access to **ThriftVision** for fast barcode scanning.
- **DashVision**: Basic stats on your scanning history.
- **AssistVision**: Access to the FAQ database.

### B. Mid Tier (Pro - $49/mo)
- **OmniVision**: Adds **ShelfVision** for scanning entire bookshelves at once.
- **OperateVision**: Full access to Inventory management and **VisionLocate** for warehouse mapping.
- **SocialVision**: Automate 7 social media posts per week to drive traffic.
- **PromoVision**: Early access to "BOLO" alerts (Be On the Look Out).

### C. Top Tier (Enterprise - $99/mo)
- **OmniVision**: Adds **SpatialVision** (AR) for real-time price overlays in-store.
- **AssistVision**: Adds **ChatVision** (Gemini AI) for specialized sourcing advice.
- **NexusVision**: Advanced multi-channel sync with automated re-pricing.
- **ProfitVision**: Detailed tax and profit analytics dashboard.

## 3. Core Workflows

### The "Source-to-List" Flow
1. **Source**: Use OmniVision (Thrift or Shelf) to scan books at a thrift store or library.
2. **Analyze**: View real-time pricing and ROI in the scan overlay.
3. **List**: Send profitable items directly to Listings via NexusVision.
4. **Locate**: Assign the physical book to a shelf location using VisionLocate.

### The "Marketing" Flow
1. **Generate**: Use SocialVision to generate AI-powered captions and images for your latest "finds".
2. **Schedule**: Set your weekly posting schedule.
3. **Engage**: Monitor performance in the DashVision marketing tab.

## 4. Support
Need help? Go to **AssistVision** for our searchable knowledge base or to chat with our AI assistant. Enterprise users can also open a "Live Agent" ticket for priority support.
